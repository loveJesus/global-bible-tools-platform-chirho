## General Rules

### Postpositive Conjunctions
Greek postpositives (words that appear second in clause but translate first):

| Greek | Lemma | Spanish | Notes |
|-------|-------|---------|-------|
| γάρ | G1063 | pues | Lighter than "porque", works mid-sentence |
| δέ | G1161 | y/pero | y for continuation, pero for contrast |
| μέν | G3303 | ciertamente | Often paired with δέ |
| τε | G5037 | también | Enclitic "and/also" |
| οὖν | G3767 | pues/entonces | Therefore/so |

### Articles
| Greek | Spanish | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| ὁ, ἡ, τό | el/la/lo | With n-dash when prefixed to noun |
| τοῖς, ταῖς | los/las | Dative plural → "a-los/a-las" |

## Translator Notes

### 1 John Translation (2026-01-19)

**Key theological vocabulary in 1 John:**
- ἀγάπη (agapē) → amor - Central theme, appears ~18 times
- κοινωνία (koinōnia) → comunión - Fellowship with God and believers
- φῶς/σκοτία → luz/tiniebla - Light/darkness contrast theme
- μένω → permanecer - "Abide/remain" - key Johannine verb
- Παράκλητος → Abogado - 1 Jn 2:1 refers to Christ as advocate
- ἱλασμός → propiciación - Atoning sacrifice (2:2, 4:10)
- χρῖσμα → unción - Anointing from Holy One (2:20, 27)

**v1:1 - Opening relative clause:**
The neuter singular ὃ (what) referring to the Word made flesh:
"Lo-que era desde el-principio" (What was from the beginning)

**v2:1 - Παράκλητος:**
Used "Abogado" (Advocate) rather than "Consolador" since context is legal intercession.

**v2:18-19 - Antichrist theme:**
- ἀντίχριστος → anticristo (singular and plural)
- Used "han-surgido" for γεγόνασιν (have arisen)

**v3:12 - Káïn:**
Transliterated with diaeresis: Káïn (maintaining Greek spelling distinction)

**v4:8, 16 - "God is love":**
ὁ Θεὸς ἀγάπη ἐστίν → "el Dios amor es" - Preserved Greek word order

**v5:6-8 - Johannine Comma:**
Included bracketed text {en el cielo...} following manuscript tradition notation.
Main witnesses: "el Espíritu y el agua y la sangre"

**v5:7 - οὐρανῷ:**
Translated as "cielo" (heaven) - standard Spanish term.

### Jude Translation (2026-01-19)

**v4 - γάρ handling:**
Used "pues" instead of "porque" because it's lighter and works mid-sentence:
"se-infiltraron pues algunos hombres" (crept-in so some men)
No brackets needed - "pues" flows naturally in Spanish.

**v5 - δέ contrast:**
Used "pero" for contrastive δέ: "recordar pero a-vosotros quiero"

**v8 - μέν...δέ construction:**
- σάρκα μὲν μιαίνουσιν → "carne ciertamente contaminan"
- κυριότητα δὲ ἀθετοῦσιν → "señorío pero rechazan"

**v11 - Exclamation:**
οὐαὶ αὐτοῖς! → "¡ay de-ellos!" (Woe to-them!)

**v22-23 - Textual variants:**
Followed NA28 text structure for the μέν...δέ...δέ sequence.

## Spanish-Specific Patterns

### Genitive Chains
Greek genitive chains become "de" chains:
- τοῦ Κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ → "del-Señor de-nosotros Iēsoûs Christós"

### Verbal Participles
Present participles often become gerunds:
- λέγων → diciendo (saying)
- ποιούμενος → haciendo (doing/making)

### Dative of Advantage
Dative "for" often becomes "para":
- τοῖς ἁγίοις → para-los-santos (for the saints)
