## Amharic-Specific Patterns

### Word Order
Amharic is SOV (Subject-Object-Verb), but for interlinear translation, we preserve
the Hebrew source word order. Each Hebrew word gets one Amharic gloss.

### Construct Chains (Semikut)
Hebrew construct chains are rendered with "–የ" (of):
- בן-אברהם → ልጅ–የ–አብርሃም (son-of-Abraham)

### Verbal Stems
Amharic verbs conjugate differently, but for interlinear we use citation/base forms:
- Active: ፈጠረ (created), አለ (said)
- Passive: ተፈጠረ (was created)
- Causative: አስፈጠረ (caused to create)

### Definite Article
Hebrew definite article ה is rendered as –ያ or –ው suffix:
- הארץ → –ያ–ምድር (the-earth)
- השמים → –ያ–ሰማያት (the-heavens)
