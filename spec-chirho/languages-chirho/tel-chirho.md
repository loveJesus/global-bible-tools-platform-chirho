### Case Suffixes (Telugu Agglutination)
- -యొక్క/-కి (genitive "of")
- -ను/-ని (accusative)
- -కు/-కి (dative "to/for")
- -లో (locative "in")
- -తో (instrumental "with")
- -నుండి (ablative "from")

## Grammar Notes

1. **Word Order**: SOV (Dravidian) — verb final, agglutinative
2. **Gender**: Three genders (masculine, feminine, neuter)
3. **Honorifics**: వారు (he-formal/respectful plural) — use for God, Jesus, prophets
4. **Sandhi**: Apply Telugu sandhi rules at word boundaries
5. **Verbal Conjugation**: Tense-person-number suffixes must be correct
