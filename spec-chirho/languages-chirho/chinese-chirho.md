## Translation Principles

### Word-for-Word Interlinear
1. Each Hebrew word gets exactly one Chinese translation
2. Same lemma should receive the same Chinese translation (consistency)
3. Particles are hyphenated with n-dash (–)
4. Use simplified Chinese characters throughout
5. Preserve Hebrew source word order for interlinear alignment
